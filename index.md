# JARVIS Legal Backup

**Private Encrypted Backup Utility**

JARVIS Legal Backup is a private utility used by its owner to maintain encrypted backups of the JARVIS Legal OS database and related documents.

The utility uses Google Drive solely to store and verify encrypted backup archives.

## How it works

- Backup files are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.
- Access is authorized by the Google account owner.
- Backup files are not made publicly accessible through this website.
- The utility is intended for private administrative use, not public registration.

## Privacy and security

This website is informational only. It does not provide access to the JARVIS Legal OS dashboard, client records, documents, or backup credentials.

For information about how Google Drive access is used, please refer to the [Privacy Policy](privacy.md).
