# Privacy Policy — JARVIS Legal Backup

**Last updated: 13 October 2026**

## 1. Purpose

JARVIS Legal Backup is a privately operated utility designed to create and store encrypted backups of the JARVIS Legal OS database and associated documents.

It is intended for use by its authorized owner and is not offered as a public service.

## 2. Google Drive Access

The utility requests Google Drive authorization to upload, list, retrieve and verify encrypted backup files.

Google Drive access is used only for backup storage, verification and recovery activities authorized by the account owner.

## 3. Information Processed

The utility processes backup archives, associated technical metadata and the Google Drive authorization credentials necessary to perform its functions.

Backup archives are encrypted before being uploaded to Google Drive.

## 4. Data Sharing

The operator does not sell Google user data or share it with advertisers.

Data may be processed by the infrastructure providers necessary to operate the backup service, including Google Drive, subject to their applicable terms and privacy practices.

## 5. Storage and Security

Google Drive credentials are intended to be stored in restricted-access configuration and automation secrets.

Encrypted backup files are intended to remain accessible only to authorized accounts. This informational website does not provide access to backup archives or client records.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Backup files are retained according to the operator's backup retention practices. The authorized owner may delete stored backup archives and revoke Google account access to the utility.
